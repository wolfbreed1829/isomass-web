03 / SCORING RULES · VERSION 1

Understanding comes before speed.

Complete every guided step to earn 70 points. Start with a 30-point efficiency bonus; each structural move beyond the perfect route costs 5 bonus points. A completed run always earns at least 70 and at most 100.

Score = 70 + max(0, 30 − 5 × extra moves)
For equal pieces: 3 moves earns 100; 4 earns 95; 5 earns 90; 9 or more earns 70.

  • What counts as a structural move? Divide, Rejoin family, Start from a whole, Undo, or Redo when it changes the partition.
  • What is free? Selecting a region, pinning the guided comparison, reading instructions, and taking a break. Elapsed time is recorded for your reference and does not affect points or rank.
  • What counts toward the total? Your best submitted score for each challenge. Replaying can improve a best; repeated runs do not accumulate points. Maximum: 300.
  • How do ties work? Equal totals share the same rank. Speed is never a tiebreaker.
  • Can I restart? Yes. Restart creates a fresh attempt; earlier completed results remain in your local log. Starting forms and saved forms are unavailable during a guide so each run starts from the same puzzle.
  • How are submissions checked? The server replays recorded actions from the fixed starting form and calculates the score itself. This checks a valid solution, not whether a human completed it without assistance.

Up to 1,000 actions can be recorded in one attempt. If you reach the limit, restart before submitting. Guides completed before scoring was introduced must be replayed to earn a recorded score.

Your name, your results.

Use an alias you are happy to display publicly: 3–24 letters, numbers, spaces, underscores, or hyphens. Names are unique without regard to letter case. A private passphrase of at least 8 characters protects your name. Keep it safe; there is no email recovery.

Your public entry shows your name, rank, and best points by challenge. Scores stay on your device until you submit them. Your passphrase is sent over HTTPS and stored as a salted hash on the server; the browser keeps a sign-in token for up to 90 days. Sign out on a shared device.

The server stores submitted attempt identifiers, move counts, and a fingerprint of each recorded run. It uses temporary request counters to limit abuse. It does not publish your passphrase, sign-in token, or elapsed time. Your local log keeps recent attempts and personal bests; download a copy before clearing browser data.
