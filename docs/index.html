    A QUICK GUIDE

    Three things to try.

    1. Divide a piece.

      Its capacity halves: 64 becomes 32 + 32. The total stays 64. Rejoin brings the entire parent family back together.

    2. Change the focus.

      Choose an arc or use the region picker. Adjacent pieces of the same size connect in teal and receive twice the weight in the field.

    3. Compare a different structure.

      Try Balanced, then Cascade. The total is the same, but the scales and connections change the pattern. Undo lets you compare edits.

    Each region contributes a shifted triangular wave. The portrait wraps the resulting samples around a circle; the trace lays them out in a line. Amber dots mark equal-size regions reflected across the partition.

    This browser edition computes locally and checks conservation at runtime. The original Idris proof and Flix/Bend runtimes belong to the separate desktop edition. This is a mathematical playground, not a physical simulation.
